Where good company meets great chai

Guftugu (गुफ्तगू) means conversation — and that is exactly what this cafe is built around. Tucked away near Amity University in Lucknow, it is a warm, aesthetic retreat from the everyday rush.

Step inside and you will find soft lighting, cozy corners, and an atmosphere that turns one cup of chai into three. Whether you are on a date, in a work meeting, or just need somewhere peaceful to think, a seat is waiting for you.
